What We Offer

Barangay Services

Documents and programs available to all Upi residents.

Barangay Clearance

Issued for employment, business, or legal purposes. Processing takes 1 business day.

Indigency Certificate

For residents seeking financial assistance from government agencies or scholarships.

Residency Certificate

Official proof of residency for school enrollment, legal, and civil transactions.

Health Center

Free consultations, vaccinations, maternal care, and medicine for qualified residents.

Day Care Center

Free early childhood education for children aged 3–5 under the Barangay Day Care Program.

Livelihood Programs

Skills training, seed capital assistance, and cooperative formation for residents.

Senior Citizens

Social pension, medicine assistance, and recreation programs for senior constituents.

Lupon Tagapamayapa

Barangay mediation and settlement of disputes to promote community harmony.
